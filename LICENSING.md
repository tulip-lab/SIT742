## Datasets and Third-Party Resources

Datasets, images, model files, archives, extracts, linked resources, and other third-party materials may have their own licences, terms, attribution requirements, or use restrictions. Their presence in this repository does not mean that the repository authors can relicense them.

Before redistributing or adapting a dataset:

1. identify its original source;
2. verify the applicable licence or permission;
3. retain required attribution and notices;
4. check privacy, platform, and research-use restrictions;
5. record unresolved cases instead of assuming the MIT License applies.

The current practical-data inventory is in [Jupyter/data/README.md](Jupyter/data/README.md). Dataset-level provenance and licensing remain incomplete and require source-specific review.
